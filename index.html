About this vaccination plan

This is India’s UIP schedule, not a private-clinic prescription. The pediatrician may recommend additional vaccines or a catch-up plan. Use the hospital vaccination card as the record of what was given.

Japanese encephalitis vaccines apply in endemic districts. Vitamin A is a separate child-health intervention, not a vaccine; ask your health worker about it. Also discuss additional vaccines with the pediatrician. India’s 2026 HPV campaign targets 14-year-old girls; review eligibility when appropriate.

Before going home

  • Collect the discharge summary and vaccination card; check birth-dose entries.
  • Ask for help with feeding, keeping baby warm, and clean cord care.
  • Write down the pediatrician’s contact and the follow-up appointment.
  • Ask about birth registration and the child’s U-WIN immunization record.
When to seek prompt medical care

Get prompt medical care for poor feeding, reduced activity, difficult breathing, fever, convulsions or a baby who feels cold. Don’t wait for the next scheduled visit.
