About this vaccination plan

Display one plan at a time. The hospital card and UIP use different groupings and ages; combination vaccines can cover several entries. Do not add the two plans together as extra doses. Confirm products, eligibility and actual appointment dates with the pediatrician.

Japanese encephalitis vaccines apply in endemic districts. Vitamin A is a separate child-health intervention, not a vaccine; ask your health worker about it. Also discuss additional vaccines with the pediatrician. India’s 2026 HPV campaign targets 14-year-old girls; review eligibility when appropriate.

One family calendar

Doctor follow-ups, vaccination visits, daily care and medicine reminders in one download. Recorded vaccines are left out.

Doctors: 14, 7, 3, 2 and 1 days before. Vaccines: 7, 3, 2 and 1 days before. Advance alerts are at 9 AM India time; past alert dates cannot notify retroactively. For planning windows, alerts use the first date.

For Google Calendar, import the file on a laptop in Settings → Import & export, then sync to Android. Opening an ICS file directly in the Android app may fail.

Calendar alerts become active only after import and sync. Check notification permission and the imported event’s reminders in Google Calendar. These are calendar notifications, not Android Clock alarms.

Replacing an older calendar?

Remove the older imported events or calendar before importing this one. A new file cannot cancel reminders already stored in Google Calendar. Until birth is confirmed, baby-care dates are labelled provisional.

Useful reminders

  • Collect the discharge summary and vaccination card; check birth-dose entries.
  • Ask for help with feeding, keeping baby warm, and clean cord care.
  • Write down the pediatrician’s contact and the follow-up appointment.
  • Ask about birth registration and the child’s U-WIN immunization record.
When to seek prompt medical care

Get prompt medical care for poor feeding, reduced activity, difficult breathing, fever, convulsions or a baby who feels cold. Don’t wait for the next scheduled visit.
